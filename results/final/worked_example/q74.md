# Worked example: GSM8K question 74

**Question.** Jenna has 4 roommates. Each month the electricity bill is $100. How much will each roommate pay per year for electricity, if they divide the share equally?

Correct answer 240; the most common wrong answer 300 divides by the 4 roommates and forgets Jenna herself.

Answer frequencies over the 10,000 stored samples (data/processed/GSM8K_Llama-3-8B-Instruct.npz): 300: 82.2%, 240: 10.0%, 1200: 2.8%, cluster 3: 2.4%.

3B judge verdicts on the representative solutions (data/processed/GSM8K_Llama-3-8B-Instruct_judge.csv): 300: 0/8 PASS, 240: 0/8 PASS, 1200: 0/8 PASS. The judge rejects the correct answer as often as the wrong ones, so its verdict carries no information on this question.

**Model.** Belief model of CV fold 2, fit on the 102 questions of the other four folds (q74 excluded). Costs: λ = 2, c_s = 0.01, c_j = 0.02. Judge Beta priors fit on the training questions: wrong answers Beta(0.52, 1.48), correct answers Beta(0.92, 1.08).

Episodes are test episodes 0-2 of eval/final.py (same seeds), so both policies see the same sample order. Belief = the DP's P(leader correct) given votes and judge verdicts (for fixed-k it is shown for reference only; fixed-k does not use it). Votes column = a-b (leader-runner-up); judge column = PASS/FAIL verdicts on the current leader.
